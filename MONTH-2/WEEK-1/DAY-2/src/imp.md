# Space Complexity & Case Analysis — Important Notes

> Month 2 · Week 1 · Day 2 | Quick revision sheet

## Must Remember
- Auxiliary space = extra memory used by algorithm; total space may include input storage.
- Count arrays/maps/sets, temporary buffers, and recursion call stack.
- In-place algorithms typically use O(1) auxiliary space (excluding input).
- Best/average/worst cases describe input-dependent work; space can also vary by case.

## Complexity / Correctness
- Identify dominant operations and count auxiliary storage separately from input.
- State preconditions and explain why the chosen operation/algorithm is valid.
- Prefer the simplest data structure that matches required operations.

## Common Traps
- Reference-vs-value confusion, mutation assumptions, index boundaries, and integer overflow.
- Test empty/minimum, typical, boundary, and invalid inputs.
