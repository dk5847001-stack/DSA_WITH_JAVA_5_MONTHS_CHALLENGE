# if/else Conditional Statements — Daily Knowledge

> MONTH-1 · WEEK-1 · DAY-5 | Knowledge journal

## Today's Knowledge
- Conditions must evaluate to boolean; use `if`, `else if`, `else` for mutually exclusive branches.
- Order ranges carefully (e.g. highest grade first); make boundaries inclusive/exclusive explicitly.
- Use `&&` for both conditions and `||` for either; avoid accidental assignment/comparison confusion.
